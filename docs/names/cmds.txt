# CMDS (cmds.c, cmds2.c, cmdsb.c): the commands Hole up / camp, Board, Xit, Fire, Ignite torch, New order, Yell (shadowlords' names,
# words of power, shrine mantras), Push/pull, Escape, Mix reagents and Klimb. One overlay, three objects (MSC 5.1, 5.1, 5.0).
# functions
CMDS_0000 CMDS_0000_HoleUpRest        # the rest itself: the party sleeps for some hours, one in 64 hours brings an ambush (a combat); 1 on an ambush, else the party heals
CMDS_06ee CMDS_06ee_RequireOnFoot     # 1 when the party is on foot (Avatar tile); else "On foot" and 0
CMDS_070c CMDS_070c_CanBoardShip      # 1 when the party is on foot, on a carpet or on a skiff; else "On foot" and 0
CMDS_073e CMDS_073e_IsLandNearby      # 1 when one of the four cells around the party is land
CMDS_0788 CMDS_0788_IsLandAt          # 1 when the view cell is land: a walkable tile, or a ground actor tile
CMDS_0962 CMDS_0962_FireBroadsides    # on the overworld a frigate fires to either side (not ahead): the first moving actor in three cells is hit (damage 1 to 20) and may be killed
CMDS_1030 CMDS_1030_YellShadowlord    # a shadowlord's name (FAULINEI, ASTAROTH, NOSFENTOR) yelled on maps 0x1e to 0x20 calls that shadowlord up as an NPC
CMDS_1202 CMDS_1202_RestoreShrine     # at ruins: the virtue and its three mantras, spoken right, restore the destroyed shrine
CMDS_12c8 CMDS_12c8_YellWordOfPower   # an overworld word of power (FALLAX ... VERAMOCOR) opens or closes the shrine or dungeon entrance beside the party, or restores a shrine at ruins
CMDS_14ba_Pushable CMDS_14ba_IsPushable   # 1 for the tiles that can be pushed: chairs, desk, barrel, vanity, dresser, trunk, cannons ...
CMDS_1504 CMDS_1504_FacingTile        # the chair or cannon tile turned for a push (or a pull) in the direction (dx, dy)
CMDS_1548_Pushed CMDS_1548_PutPushed  # puts a pushed object on its new cell and the empty tile behind, turning chairs and cannons; "Pushed!"
CMDS_15b0_Pulled CMDS_15b0_PutPulled  # the same for a pulled object; "Pulled!"
CMDS_18be CMDS_18be_SelectReagents    # the reagent list with a cursor: keys move, return toggles, M accepts, Esc aborts; the bit mask of the chosen reagents (-1 aborted)
CMDS_1a70 CMDS_1a70_AskMixAmount      # "How much?" until the amount is covered by every chosen reagent; the amount
