# DUNGEON (dungeon.c + dungeonb.c): the dungeons: the command loop, walking and turning, traps and fields, the wandering
# monster of the level, entering a room (a fight on a combat map from DUNGEON.CBT), klimbing, and the first-person view
# (walls, doors, fields, ladders, the monster). One overlay: dungeon.c is MSC 5.1, dungeonb.c (from DUNGEON_1020 or
# DUNGEON_104c) MSC 5.0. DUNGEON_1952 has a stand-in device (its note): its locals are renamed through the tool only.
# functions
DUNGEON_0000 DUNGEON_0000_EnterRoom             # "Entering room...": a cell of kind 0xa? / 0xf? opens its room: the combat map is read from dungeon.cbt, the party fights; a won room is marked cleared
DUNGEON_0134 DUNGEON_0134_SetUpMonster          # the level's wandering monster (actor 1): with the flag, a new one (type of D_173c by a roll of 0-7, placed at random) and its picture loaded
DUNGEON_0252 DUNGEON_0252_PlaceMonster          # puts actors 1 and 2 on a random open cell (eight tries, not on the party's row and column); 1 when it found one
DUNGEON_0332 DUNGEON_0332_DrawFrame             # the view's top and bottom bars and the labels "L " (level) and "Dir:"
DUNGEON_03d6 DUNGEON_03d6_WaitForKey            # the prompt; while no key is there the view is redrawn (light flicker); returns the key (arrow keys as 0xfa+)
DUNGEON_0502 DUNGEON_0502_WalkCmd               # Advance / Turn right / Back up / Turn left / Turn around: blocked by walls and doors, an electric field (0x83) hurts; 1 when it moved
DUNGEON_07e2 DUNGEON_07e2_MoveMonster           # the wandering monster takes a random step (not into pits, fields or walls); 1 when it reaches the party
DUNGEON_0a4c DUNGEON_0a4c_FallThroughPit        # a pit trap: falls a level at a time while the cell below is a pit too, "splat" damage; a room cell on arrival is entered
DUNGEON_0b7e DUNGEON_0b7e_MonsterAttacks        # "Attacked from the ...!": the party turns to face the monster, then a fight
DUNGEON_0c76 DUNGEON_0c76_AfterStep             # after a command that moved: sleepers may wake, the monster moves, a room is entered, a trap or field acts, the turn ends
DUNGEON_1020 DUNGEON_1020_StepSound             # every other step a noise of volume D_a9fb that dies down by 4
DUNGEON_104c DUNGEON_104c_ShowPlaceName         # the name of a special place of this dungeon (D_2e10) in a small window on the view
DUNGEON_10dc DUNGEON_10dc_CellAt                # the cell of this level at (x, y) wrapped to 8x8; bit 3 (visited) cleared below 0x90
DUNGEON_111e DUNGEON_111e_DrawMonster           # the wandering monster at a distance of 1-3: its frames (size, mirroring, animation by its state) as pictures
DUNGEON_127e DUNGEON_127e_DrawField             # a field at that distance: random short lines in the field's colour (sleep magenta, poison green, fire red, energy blue)
DUNGEON_134a DUNGEON_134a_DrawPiece             # one piece of the view (a wall part from DNGn.16, or from ITEMS.16 above 0x1f), plain or mirrored, at the place of its distance
DUNGEON_145c DUNGEON_145c_DrawDrip              # the drip of a stalactite in its phases (0-5); phase 5 is the sound; the next phase is returned
DUNGEON_150a DUNGEON_150a_DrawAhead             # the cell straight ahead at that distance: its pieces, drips, the place's name; 1 when it is open and the view goes on
DUNGEON_1682 DUNGEON_1682_DrawSideCell          # a cell beside the corridor (side 0 left, 1 right) at that distance
DUNGEON_1786 DUNGEON_1786_DrawWallDots          # the dots of a ladder's rungs (or a hole) at that distance, one set per frame
DUNGEON_1952 DUNGEON_1952_DrawCell              # a cell at a distance, from the far end back: wall pieces, ladders, fields, the visited mark, the monster there
DUNGEON_1a90 DUNGEON_1a90_DrawView              # renders the first-person view into page 1: the cells in front (up to four, in light), then back to front; dark without a torch or light
DUNGEON_1be0 DUNGEON_1be0_Redraw                # vitals, the view to the screen, step sound, level and direction
DUNGEON_1c0c DUNGEON_1c0c_CanLandOn             # whether the cell under the party on that level is free (the logic is probably not what was meant)
#= DUNGEON_01d2_PrintWalkDir # prints the level number and the facing (North, East, South, West) in the view's bars

#= DUNGEON_06c4_ProcessCommand # one key of the dungeon: Ctrl-K karma, E exit, V version, S sound, movement, a number selects a player, else the common commands

#= DUNGEON_1d08_Exit # leaves the dungeon to the surface or the underworld, at the dungeon's place on the map
#= DUNGEON_1d4a_AttackCmd # the Attack command: fights the monster when it is in the cell ahead
#= DUNGEON_1e10_KlimbCmd # the Klimb command: a ladder or the grapple allows up, down or both (then asks U/D)
