# SJOG (sjog.c): the commands Search, Jimmy, Open and Get (on the map and in a dungeon), what a chest holds and what
# lies hidden on the maps (the table D_3e78..D_40b2), and the helpers of combat: moving a player, leaving the field,
# klimbing, the end of a combat, whether a cell is blocked, charm. One overlay, MSC 5.0.
# functions
SJOG_0000 SJOG_0000_FindFreeActor              # the last free actor slot (tile 0), counting down from 31; 0 when none

SJOG_02ea SJOG_02ea_SearchChestTrap            # searching a chest: the member's intelligence against the chest's trap value tells "no trap", "a simple trap", "a complex trap" or just "a trap"
SJOG_03a8 SJOG_03a8_SearchMoonstone            # a buried moonstone (the tables D_5830..D_5848) at this place and map becomes an actor; 1 when found

SJOG_0514 SJOG_0514_FindHiddenItem             # looks the place up in the table of hidden items (D_3f5c..D_40b2: map, level, x, y, tile, value); a find that is not taken yet becomes an actor, else "nothing of note"
SJOG_0baa SJOG_0baa_JimmyChest                 # picking a chest's lock with a key: a locked one (value >= 0x80) opens by dexterity, else the key is used up
SJOG_0f88 SJOG_0f88_DropItem                   # puts one item actor of a chest's contents at the place: the amount by kind and level (kinds 1 and 2 random), and what it is is printed
SJOG_1040 SJOG_1040_DropTreasure               # the chest's treasure: each of the eight kinds D_4124 (gold, food, torches, gems, keys...) that the chest's level allows, by a roll
SJOG_10b8 SJOG_10b8_DropGear                   # the chest's gear: half its level in tries on the 48 entries of D_413c (weapons, armour, scrolls, potions...) by their level thresholds D_416c
SJOG_1b34 SJOG_1b34_UnequipAll                 # takes the item off every party member who has it equipped; how many did
SJOG_1b6c SJOG_1b6c_CountCombatants            # counts the living combatants: the enemies into D_5876, the party's side into D_5878
SJOG_203e SJOG_203e_EndCombat                  # the combat is over: the actors are put back; the monster's actor is removed (a pirate ship sinks to a wreck); the map id is restored
SJOG_20d8 SJOG_20d8_IsCellBlocked              # 1 when the cell (x, y) is not free for the combatant (0 off the map when it may leave)
SJOG_2148 SJOG_2148_IsSurrounded               # 1 when all four neighbouring cells of the combatant are blocked (the probing walk is as in the original)
SJOG_21ce SJOG_21ce_BreakCharm                 # the first charmed party member among the first six is freed: sound, the item 0x23 taken off, put to sleep; the index or -1
SJOG_2012 SJOG_2012_TurnUpkeep                 # at a combatant's turn: the ring, fading fields, the protection spell's turns count down
#= SJOG_0646_SearchInDungeon # Search in a dungeon: what the cell ahead hides: traps (by level and dexterity), pits, bombs, hidden doors, fields, treasure
#= SJOG_095c_SearchCmd # the Search command: in a dungeon its own; else a direction, then a chest's trap, a body, a hidden door, a moonstone, reagents, or an item of the hidden table
#= SJOG_0c3e_JimmyInDungeon # Jimmy in a dungeon: a locked chest (0x4?) opens or loses a key, by dexterity and level
#= SJOG_0d4a_JimmyCmd # the Jimmy command: a key opens a locked door (dexterity), frees a prisoner behind bars (84, 85: karma + 2, the NPC changed), or picks a chest
#= SJOG_112c_OpenChest # opens a chest: the trap (value 0x80 up) springs, the treasure and the gear drop; karma falls by 2 in a town
#= SJOG_12d4_OpenInDungeon # Open in a dungeon: a chest (0x4?) springs its trap and becomes an open one (0x7?)
#= SJOG_1374_OpenCmd # the Open command: in a dungeon its own; else a direction, then a door (the open door shuts again after four turns), a trunk, or a chest
#= SJOG_1458_GetObject # takes an object (tile kind, amount, actor slot) into the party's goods: food, gold, torches, gems, keys, scrolls, potions, gear, the shards, crown, sceptre, amulet, a carpet, the sandalwood box; the actor is removed
#= SJOG_179e_GetInDungeon # Get in a dungeon: an open chest (0x7?) gives its treasure, by level
#= SJOG_18ce_GetCmd # the Get command: in a dungeon its own; else a direction, then an object there, a torch from a wall, or food from crops and tables
#= SJOG_1bb2_CombatExit # a combatant leaves the field (not on a ship): the first player's direction fixes where the party leaves; 1 when it left
#= SJOG_1c56_CombatMovePlayer # moves a combatant of the party one cell in a direction: off the map it leaves; a blocked cell sounds a tone; 1 when it moved
#= SJOG_1d6a_CombatKlimb # Klimb in combat: a ladder up or down leaves the field (up 5, down 6); or one step across a 0x4c tile
#= SJOG_1ea4_CheckMirror # a combatant who steps on the row-2 mirror of the endgame room leaves the field (exit code 0x4d)
#= SJOG_1f26_CombatMiscCmd # a command the world has and combat refuses: prints its word, a message, two tones; always 1
#= SJOG_1f7a_CombatSetActivePlayer # a number key in combat selects that party member as the active player when he or she can act; 1 when it did
