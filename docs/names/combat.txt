# COMBAT (combat.c): the combat turn loop, the monsters' turns (target, move, attack), the player's
# command, damage and the stats of a combatant. One overlay. Public ones are called from the spells,
# COMSUBS, SJOG and the commands.
# functions
COMBAT_0000 COMBAT_0000_IsCellFree             # a tile may be entered at (x, y) of the 11x11 map: walkable, no actor or combatant on it (off the map: 1)
COMBAT_014e COMBAT_014e_MonsterRanged          # a monster's missile or spell at a target (half of the time; a magic monster not at all when magic is negated)
COMBAT_0226 COMBAT_0226_MonsterAttack          # a monster attacks the target it chose: in reach it strikes, farther it shoots; 1 when it acted
COMBAT_03f4 COMBAT_03f4_MonsterTurn            # a monster's whole turn: asleep, held, special tricks, attack or move, escape off the view
COMBAT_0544 COMBAT_0544_RunCommand             # prints the command's word and runs it for a party member (Get, Jimmy, Open, Ready, Search, Use); a monster "Can't!"
COMBAT_05b6 COMBAT_05b6_AppendWeaponName       # adds ", " and the weapon's name to the line in D_b21e_scratch; 1 when it has one
COMBAT_0d30 COMBAT_0d30_ChooseTarget           # the nearest opponent (the party's side or the monsters'); D_5876/D_5878 get the step toward it (away when fleeing)
COMBAT_0ee4 COMBAT_0ee4_MonsterMove            # a teleporting monster may jump; otherwise a step toward the target, else a random one; 1 when it moved
COMBAT_111a COMBAT_111a_FireMapTrigger         # a trigger cell of the combat map (D_58a1_combatFlags & 0x82) changes the tiles it controls; 1 when it did
COMBAT_120e COMBAT_120e_RandomCell             # a random cell of the 16x16 into D_5876/D_5878; 1 when it is on the 11x11 map
COMBAT_1236 COMBAT_1236_RemoveEntity           # clears an actor (index + 1) or, negative, a combatant (-(index + 1)) and its actor
COMBAT_12b0 COMBAT_12b0_RollDamage             # the damage of a hit: the weapon's (monster: its attack) minus a random roll of the victim's defense; 99 kills at once
COMBAT_139a COMBAT_139a_EffectiveDex           # 1 for the held, the asleep and the Gazer (0x1a); else the combatant's dexterity
COMBAT_13e2 COMBAT_13e2_QueryStat              # intelligence, dexterity, strength or defense of a combatant; a weapon number picks strength or dexterity
COMBAT_14d6 COMBAT_14d6_RollToHit              # (target dexterity - attacker's stat + 30) / 2 against a roll of 1 to 30; spells use intelligence; 1 when it hits
COMBAT_1574 COMBAT_1574_ApplyDamage            # hurts a combatant: a party member dies at 0, a monster leaves a corpse or chest, vanishes, or divides; returns the experience
COMBAT_18ba COMBAT_18ba_Poison                 # poisons a party member, or hurts any other by up to 20 (the attacker gets the experience)
COMBAT_194a COMBAT_194a_ResolveHit             # what a landed hit does: poison, sleep, the weapon's damage; the party's attacker earns the experience
COMBAT_1a5c COMBAT_1a5c_WoundLevel             # how hurt a monster is, 1 (a quarter left) to 4; a badly hurt monster may start to flee
COMBAT_1b1e COMBAT_1b1e_TerrainEffect          # the cell a combatant ends its turn on: fire and lava burn, a poison field poisons, a sleep field sleeps
COMBAT_1c66 COMBAT_1c66_Regurgitate            # a swallowed combatant may be brought up again, by its dexterity
