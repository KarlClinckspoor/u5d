# INTRO (intro0.c, intro.c, intro2.c, introb.c): the introduction and main menu of the game: the title screens (Origin
# logo, Lord British drawn by a path, ULTIMA, "Warriors of Destiny"), the menu (Journey Onward, Create New Character,
# Transfer from Ultima IV, Ultima V Introduction, Acknowledgements, Return to the View), the story pages (STORY.DAT),
# and the transfer of an Ultima IV character (INTRO_132a is assembly in the DOS build, AINTRO2.ASM). One overlay, four
# objects. The three const tables D_306c, D_30c4 and D_30da stay as they are: intro.c and introb.c rename them out of the way.
# functions
INTRO_0010 INTRO_0010_PutBackdropStrips    # on page 1: the four 50-line pictures 1..4 of ULTIMA.16 stacked at x 0x10 (probably the backdrop of the menu and story pages)
INTRO_043e INTRO_043e_PrintCaptionBar      # a text centred in a bar closed by caps at the bottom of the screen (scene titles, the copyright line)
INTRO_12ea INTRO_12ea_ScaleU4Stat          # an Ultima IV stat (up to 50) on Ultima V's scale (up to 30): below 10 as is, up to 29 by halves, then by quarters
INTRO_1e22 INTRO_1e22_PrintFieldLabel      # the label of one of eight fields ("Name:" ... "INT:") in both windows of the transfer screen
INTRO_1e62 INTRO_1e62_DrawPromptBox        # the framed box at the bottom of the transfer screen (rows 19 to 22)
INTRO_1f26 INTRO_1f26_DrawCharacterBox     # a framed " Ultima IV " box for one of the two characters of the transfer screen, at an x offset
INTRO_2024 INTRO_2024_SetUpTransferScreen  # the transfer screen: the prompt box, three text windows, the two character boxes (old character at left, new at right)
