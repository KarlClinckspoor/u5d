# LOOKOBJ (lookobj.c): the Look command (what is at a place: tiles, signs, wells, fountains, the sky, creatures)
# and the "view" (a vision of the whole 32x32 map drawn four pixels a cell). One overlay, MSC 5.0.
# functions
LOOKOBJ_0000 LOOKOBJ_0000_PrintTileName          # the description of a tile, from LOOK2.DAT (offset table at tile * 2)
LOOKOBJ_0042 LOOKOBJ_0042_Well                   # "a well": drop a coin and wish; a vehicle's name (Corvette, Horse...) in towns 0x16 or 0x1f makes a horse appear
LOOKOBJ_0162 LOOKOBJ_0162_Fountain               # "a gurgling fountain": who will drink? the dead and the asleep are incapacitated, nobody is healed
LOOKOBJ_01ac LOOKOBJ_01ac_DrawStar               # a white star at the sky's cell (x, y): 3x3 pixels, with dots to each side, clipped at the right edge
LOOKOBJ_024c LOOKOBJ_024c_DrawRedMark            # a red mark (a flame shape) at the star's cell, for a planet in front of which a Shadowlord stands
LOOKOBJ_0366 LOOKOBJ_0366_LookSky                # by day "the sun!" (it burns the active player); at night draws the sky and the eight planets for the date
LOOKOBJ_0502 LOOKOBJ_0502_DescribeTile           # what a tile is: the sky, a well, a fountain, a clock (the time), the altars' words, a dungeon's name, else its LOOK2.DAT text
LOOKOBJ_06a4 LOOKOBJ_06a4_DescribeActor          # the description of the creature or person looked at (LOOK2.DAT, offset table at 0x200 + tile * 2)
LOOKOBJ_06f8 LOOKOBJ_06f8_PrintSignText          # a sign's text from the scratch buffer (-1: the default "Live by the Eight Laws" sign): frame characters, a key press at 0xd
LOOKOBJ_07e4 LOOKOBJ_07e4_ReadSign               # the sign at (level, x, y): the WANTED poster of the party in town 4, else SIGNS.DAT's text for the place
LOOKOBJ_0a9c LOOKOBJ_0a9c_SetCellOrigin          # the pixel position (D_5876/D_5878) of the vision's cell (x, y): 4 pixels a cell, at 0x20
LOOKOBJ_0abe LOOKOBJ_0abe_DrawGreenDots          # 4 green dots in a checker
LOOKOBJ_0b04 LOOKOBJ_0b04_DrawGreenBlock         # a solid light green 4x4 cell
LOOKOBJ_0b28 LOOKOBJ_0b28_DrawVertBars           # two white vertical bars
LOOKOBJ_0b60 LOOKOBJ_0b60_DrawHorizBars          # white lines along the top and bottom
LOOKOBJ_0b98 LOOKOBJ_0b98_DrawInnerBars          # two short white lines in the middle
LOOKOBJ_0bd0 LOOKOBJ_0bd0_DrawFrame              # a white frame around the cell
LOOKOBJ_0c36 LOOKOBJ_0c36_DrawYellowSteps        # yellow blocks in two corners
LOOKOBJ_0c9c LOOKOBJ_0c9c_DrawGreenStripes       # green lines on rows 0 and 2 and two dots
LOOKOBJ_0cf4 LOOKOBJ_0cf4_DrawShore              # four dots: green on the land corners of a shore tile (0x60-0x6f, D_3822 masks), water-coloured on the others
LOOKOBJ_0dda LOOKOBJ_0dda_DrawWaterDots          # two water-coloured dots (magenta on drivers 0 and 3, else light blue)
LOOKOBJ_0e16 LOOKOBJ_0e16_DrawMarsh              # two green dots and two water-coloured dots
LOOKOBJ_0e7a LOOKOBJ_0e7a_DrawEdgedBlock         # tiles 0x20-0x26: green dots, a red centre, white edges as D_3832 says and a black dot in the corner of some
LOOKOBJ_0f7e LOOKOBJ_0f7e_DrawMapCell            # draws one cell of the vision by the pattern D_1d1a gives its tile (0 nothing, 1-16 the pictures above, 90 white)

#= LOOKOBJ_10fc_ViewCmd # the vision: draws the 32x32 map around the origin four pixels a cell and blinks the party's cell until a key
# globals
D_3750 D_3750_planetStartPos        # the eight planets: the position on its orbit (index into D_3760's row) at the reference date, 5th of the 4th month of year 39
D_3758 D_3758_planetRow             # and the sky row (y cell) of each
D_3760 D_3760_orbitSteps            # per planet 22 flags of its orbit: 1 where it may stand (the walk back in time skips the 0 slots)
D_3822 D_3822_shoreLandMask         # per shore tile (0x60-0x6f): 4 bits (one per corner) set where it is water
D_3832 D_3832_edgeBits              # per tile 0x20-0x26: which of the four edges are drawn (bits 8, 4, 2, 1 = top, right, bottom, left)
D_1d1a D_1d1a_cellPattern           # tile -> the pattern that draws it in the vision (0 none, 1-16, 90)
