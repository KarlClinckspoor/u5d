# DNGLOOK (dnglook.c): the dungeon's Look and View commands, the rooms already cleared, and the combat map
# built for a fight in a dungeon (walls, openings by the neighbouring cells, who stands where), the fleeing step
# and the loading of the dungeon pictures. One overlay, MSC 5.0.
# functions
DNGLOOK_0284 DNGLOOK_0284_DrawFieldStripes     # a field cell of the overview: stripes in magenta, red, blue and green
DNGLOOK_0340 DNGLOOK_0340_DrawViewCell         # the View command: draws one cell (its glyph or picture) of the dungeon level at (col, row) of the 22x22 view; 1 when it is open ground to spread to
DNGLOOK_0844 DNGLOOK_0844_MarkRoomCleared      # sets the room's bit in D_58e0_roomsCleared (the level's 16 rooms), except for the rooms D_383a lists
DNGLOOK_08d4 DNGLOOK_08d4_IsRoomCleared        # the room's bit of D_58e0_roomsCleared on this level
DNGLOOK_093a DNGLOOK_093a_ApplyClearedRooms    # in the loaded level, a cell of kind 0xf? whose room is cleared becomes 0xa? (&= 0xaf: a door)
DNGLOOK_097e DNGLOOK_097e_FillWallSide         # a combat map side (0 north, 1 east, 2 south, 3 west) all solid (0xff); a door cell (0xe0) puts two wall tiles in the middle
DNGLOOK_0a48 DNGLOOK_0a48_OpenNarrowGap        # five floor tiles of the wall ring on one side
DNGLOOK_0aee DNGLOOK_0aee_OpenWideGap          # seven floor tiles of the wall ring on one side
DNGLOOK_0b9e DNGLOOK_0b9e_BuildSide            # the combat map's side toward the neighbouring cell: a gap when it is open, solid when it is a wall, else the narrow gap
DNGLOOK_0c6c DNGLOOK_0c6c_BuildWallRing        # the combat map's wall ring (rows and columns 1 and 9), the centre piece of the party's cell kind (D_244a) and the four sides
DNGLOOK_0d3e DNGLOOK_0d3e_SetUpDungeonFight    # floor, ring, the party's start cells, the monsters' cells (turned by the facing), the monster kind and how many; fills the combat map
DNGLOOK_0fda DNGLOOK_0fda_StepAfterCombat      # after a fight the party steps in the direction it left by (wrapping), or takes the ladder: up (5) or down (6); out of the dungeon from level 0 or 7
DNGLOOK_109e DNGLOOK_109e_LoadDungeonImages    # loads the dungeon's wall pictures (DNGn.16, n by the style) and ITEMS.16, clears the view; the encounter actor too when asked
DNGLOOK_1130 DNGLOOK_1130_FreeDungeonImages    # on leaving: frees the pictures, loads TILES.16 again and the tileset mode
DNGLOOK_117e DNGLOOK_117e_PlaceCombatants      # puts the party on the fight's start cells (by the facing) and the monsters of the combat map as actors, with their values
